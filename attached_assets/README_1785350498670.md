# Composite Scoring + Telegram Alerts

Built from `crypsor-score-log-ALL-2026-07-29.csv` (116,768 rows) only — no
assumptions carried in from anything else.

## Files
- `scoringEngine.ts` — computes a new `compositeScore` (0-100) alongside your
  existing `intel_score`, plus tags each token with `FactorTag`s (good /
  surprising / dumping).
- `telegramAlerts.ts` — turns a `ScoreResult` into a formatted alert
  (`GOOD_SETUP`, `SURPRISE_SIGNAL`, `DUMP_WARNING`) and a message string.
  Delivery is a thin `fetch` call to the Bot API — swap in your own
  BullMQ/queue wrapper.

## Why these weights
Correlation of each raw component to `score_delta` across the full log:

| component        | r      |
|-------------------|--------|
| holder_velocity   | 0.536  |
| mc_growth         | 0.160  |
| liquidity_health  | 0.032  |
| kol_smart         | 0.019  |
| vol_intensity     | 0.005  |

`holder_velocity` is by far the strongest predictor of where the score moves
next, so it gets the dominant weight (0.40). The rest are weighted down from
there but not to zero — they still matter for factor tagging even where
they're weak predictors of *movement*.

## Why these thresholds
Pulled from the log's own percentile distribution (not guessed):
- `liquidity_health` is basically bimodal at 10 / 20 — anything ≤15 is
  "thin pool," ≥20 is "healthy."
- `kol_smart`: p75=48, p90=70 → mid/high bands.
- `mc_growth`: p50=15, p90=51.9 → "hasn't moved yet" vs "already pumped."
- `vol_intensity`: p75=86.6 → "volume spike" band.
- `holder_velocity`: pump-cohort mean was 97.3, dump-cohort mean was 53.1, so
  ≥90 = strong momentum, ≤40 = clearly decelerating.

## Factor logic in plain terms
- **Good**: price/holders/liquidity moving together in a healthy way.
- **Surprising**: KOL/smart buying or holder growth spiking *before* price
  has moved — a leading indicator worth front-running.
- **Dumping**: liquidity draining under a volume spike, holders leaving
  under sell pressure, or a price pump with no holder/liquidity support
  underneath it (classic stale/rug pump).

## Not included (by design)
- No delivery/queueing wiring — you said you'll handle that.
- No DB/Drizzle schema — plug `RawSignal` in from wherever you're already
  pulling the intel_score components.
- Thresholds are static constants (`SCORING_THRESHOLDS`), not re-derived
  live — recompute them if the data distribution shifts materially.
